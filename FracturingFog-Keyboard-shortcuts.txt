Fracturing Fog Keyboard shortcuts (as of v0.6.2)
All Modes:
M: Menu
T: Theme Editor
R: Reset
V: Save Region

Non-3D fractal Types:
W: Zoom In
A: Pan Left
S: Zoom Out
D: Pan Right

3D Fractal Types (Userbulb 3D, Mandelbulb):
W: Distance-
A: Pan Left
S: Distance+
D: Pan Right
Arrow Up: Camera Phi+
Arrow Down: Camera Phi-
Arrow Left: Camera Theta-
Arrow Right: Camera Theta+
Mouse Right-click move up: Camera Phi+
Mouse Right-click move Down: Camera Phi-
Mouse Right-click move Left: Camera Theta-
Mouse Right-click move Right: Camera Theta+
Pg Up: Light Theta-
Pg Down: Light Theta+
Home: Light Phi-
End: Light Phi+
